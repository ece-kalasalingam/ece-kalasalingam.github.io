Our students work on modern technologies including VLSI design, signal processing, wireless communication, IoT, embedded systems, artificial intelligence, and photonic integrated circuits.

Research Areas

VLSI and Embedded Systems

Low-power architectures, FPGA design, hardware acceleration, RTL design, and semiconductor system design.

AI and Smart Systems

Intelligent sensing, biomedical AI, signal analysis, computer vision, and edge intelligence applications.

Communication Engineering

Wireless networks, antennas, RF systems, optical communication, and next-generation connectivity.

Photonics and Optoelectronics

Silicon photonics, photonic integrated circuits, optical sensors, and layout-level photonic design.
