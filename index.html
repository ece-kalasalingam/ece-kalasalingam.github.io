Research Focus Areas

Core engineering disciplines and emerging technology domains shaping the department.

VLSI & Embedded Systems

Digital IC design, hardware accelerators, SoC development, and embedded platform engineering.

Signal Processing & Communications

Wireless communications, adaptive filtering, image and video processing, and spectrum management.

RF & Antenna Engineering

Microwave circuits, antenna design, propagation modeling, and RF system integration.

AI-Enabled Electronics

Edge inference, hardware-software co-design, neural accelerators, and intelligent sensor systems.

Photonics & Optical Engineering

Fiber optics, optical sensors, photonic devices, and optoelectronic system design.

Control Systems & IoT

Real-time control, IoT architecture, industrial automation, and cyber-physical systems.
